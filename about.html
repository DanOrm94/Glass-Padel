About Glass Padel

Glass Padel is a modern padel facility based in Stalybridge, offering high-quality courts for players of all abilities. Whether you’re completely new to padel or an experienced player looking for competitive games, our premium glass courts provide the perfect setting to enjoy one of the world’s fastest-growing sports.

We focus on creating a welcoming, social environment where friends, families, and local players can stay active, have fun, and be part of a growing padel community.
